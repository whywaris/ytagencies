Questions agencies ask

Anything else? Email help@ytagencies.com.

Is listing really free?

Yes. A full profile and inquiries from businesses are free for as long as you like. Pro is optional.

You already listed my agency. Why?

We list agencies from public information so businesses can compare them. Claim your listing to correct anything, add your work and receive inquiries. You can also ask us to remove it.

Can I pay to rank higher?

Pro agencies are listed above Free ones, and Sponsored spots sit at the top of a page with a clear label. Inside each group, the order depends on verification, profile completeness, reviews and reply time.

How do I get the Verified badge?

Confirm a work email on your website's domain, and we review your business details. Add the channels you've worked on and we ask the channel owners to confirm. The badge is free and can't be bought.

What happens after the 6 Founding months?

We email you before it ends. If you don't choose Pro, your listing moves to Free and stays live. Nothing is charged automatically.
