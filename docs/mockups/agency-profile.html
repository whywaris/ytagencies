At a glance

Northwake Media is a London-based YouTube agency founded in 2019 with 11–50 people. It runs influencer campaigns, YouTube Shorts and video production, mostly for ecommerce, gaming and fitness brands in the UK and US. Pricing starts at $3,000 per month on a monthly retainer with a 3-month minimum; ad spend is paid separately to Google.
